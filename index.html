About Us

note1 is an AI meeting notetaker designed to make meetings easier and more productive. It keeps a record of your conversations so you can give people your full attention instead of worrying about writing everything down.

After a meeting, you can review important decisions, remember commitments, and catch up on details you may have missed without replaying the entire call. note1 helps you find the information you need when you need it.

Sharing notes with your team is simple, helping everyone stay informed and organized. Visit our official website to learn more about how note1 can make your meetings more useful.
